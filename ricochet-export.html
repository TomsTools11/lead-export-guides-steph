Export Your Leads
From Ricochet360.

A quick walkthrough for exporting your full lead list from Ricochet360 and sending it to your GOAL Account Manager.

5 Steps CSV, All Fields Send It As-Is

Export Your Leads
In 5 Steps.

  1. 1

    Open The Leads Tab

    Log in to your Ricochet360 portal. In the left sidebar, click Leads (or Search Leads) to open your leads list.

  2. 2

    Set Your Timeframe

    At the top of your leads dashboard, use the date filter to set the window your Account Manager asked for, for example Past 30 Days, Past Month or a custom range.

    You don't need to narrow it down to GOAL's campaigns. Pull the complete export for that timeframe and we'll scrub out everything else on our side.

  3. 3

    Select All The Records

    Once the filtered list appears, check the master checkbox in the top-left header of the list to select every record at once.

  4. 4

    Export To A CSV File

    In the action menu (or table tools), click Export / Generate CSV. Keep the default “All Fields” so Phone Number, Lead Name and Status/Disposition are all included. Save it to your computer as a .csv file.

  5. 5

    Email The File To Your Account Manager

    Reply to your Account Manager's email with the .csv attached.

    Don't modify, clean or rewrite anything, especially the phone numbers. They're how we tie your results back, so send the file exactly as it downloads.
