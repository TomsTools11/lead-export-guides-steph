Export Your Quote And Sale Results From Lead Manager

Export your GOAL leads from Lead Manager as a CSV or Excel file and email it to your Account Manager, exactly as it downloads.

Jump to a step

  1. 1Open Your GOAL Leads
  2. 2Set The Date Range
  3. 3Export The List To A File
  4. 4Email The File To Your Account Manager
  1. 1

    Open Your GOAL Leads

    In Lead Manager, pull up your GOAL leads. Either way works, so pick whatever is easier.

    Easiest Open Your GOAL Folder Open the folder named “GOAL”. Your GOAL leads collect here automatically.
    Or Use Your Reporting Screen Go to Reporting and filter the Source to your GOAL leads.
  2. 2

    Set The Date Range

    Set the start date to the day you started with GOAL, and the end date to today.

  3. 3

    Export The List To A File

    Click Export and download the list as a CSV or Excel file. Leave all the columns in, and send it exactly as it downloads. No need to reformat anything.

  4. 4

    Email The File To Your Account Manager

    Attach the exported file to an email and send it over.

    Send Your Export To Stephanie Ko · GOAL Account Manager stephanie@checkoutgoal.com
